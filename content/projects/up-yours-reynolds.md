During the COVID-19 pandemic, Des Moines Public Schools leadership took many lifesaving measures, often in direct opposition to Iowa Governor Kim Reynolds. I created Up Yours, Reynolds! social media content, mugs and bumper stickers to show support for DMPS and to raise funds for schools in our community.
