A small exploration game about tidepools and the creatures that live in them.

::: grid-2
![A grey, carved stone face with wide eyes, a broad nose and a grid-toothed grin on a black background](../../assets/tidepool-game-image-1.webp "Early character study")
![A pixel-art television with a grey static screen and a green channel number 3 beside its buttons](../../assets/tidepool-game-tv-static.gif "The in-game TV tuning between channels")
:::

The look of the game leans on lo-fi screens and carved textures, so the interface feels like something you found rather than something you installed.
