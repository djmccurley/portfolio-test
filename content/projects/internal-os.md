*What if virtual assistants integrated internally, interacting directly with our body and memory?*

This project investigates theory of mind, memory, mental health, human augmentation and data privacy through speculative design. Using generative AI, conversational design and audio-visual narrative, Internal OS simulates installing and interacting with an internally-hosted virtual assistant in 2124.

I created Internal OS by interacting with a [Voiceflow](https://www.voiceflow.com/) AI agent trained on fictional data, including biometrics, personal and family history, old voicemails, internal thoughts and memories and the (non-fictional) Burger King menu.
