Techno Lumina is my ongoing art project and lifestyle brand. The project is named after a real life ‘98 Chevy Lumina that used to roam the streets of Cedar Falls, Iowa blasting techno music.

*Featured vendor: 80/35 Music Festival, Market Day Iowa, Brilliant Craft and Wares Fair, The Pear Fair*
