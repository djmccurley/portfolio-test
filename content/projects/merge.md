MERGE is a co-creative experience rooted in communal sitting, interactive games and storytelling. Designed as an interactive exhibit, participants come together to create new constellations and tell original stories in an immersive environment.

Process and background: [MERGE: A Collaborative Intervention [PDF]](../../assets/s2026-artgr-6100-dmccurley-nkhoury-collaborative-interventions.pdf)

*Designed collaboratively with [Nadine Khoury](https://www.nskhoury.com/).*

[Play MERGE on itch.io](https://technolumina.itch.io/merge)
