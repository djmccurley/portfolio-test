Real grammar and lists (read this session; re-read the files, which are the source of truth):
- lib/blocks.js exports `LAYOUT_SIZES` (keys `grid-2`, `grid-3`, `grid-4`, `full`) and `EMBEDS` (Map keys `video`, `itch`). Layout blocks may contain only images. Embed blocks take no content (`:::` on the next line). Blocks cannot nest or sit inside lists or quotes. markdown-it-attrs allows only `class` and `decorative`.
- lib/embeds.js grammar:
  - `::: video [full] <url-or-assets-file> "Title" [poster=<assets-image>]`. YouTube (youtube.com, youtu.be, shorts, live, embed) and Vimeo URLs are accepted. `poster=` is allowed only for files, and files must be .mp4 or .webm.
  - `::: itch [full] <embed-url> <W>x<H> "Title" <itch-page-url>`. The embed host must be itch.io, *.itch.io or *.itch.zone, and the page host itch.io or *.itch.io, both https.
- lib/markdown.js: an image title becomes the caption (`![alt](path "Caption")`). `{decorative}` after an image marks it decorative (empty alt allowed, no warning). A plain markdown link to an `assets/*.pdf` becomes a `/pdf/` link with ` (PDF, <size>)` appended. A titled image-only paragraph outside a block becomes a figure.
- lib/validate.js (after plans 04-01 and 04-02):
  - `PROJECT_KEYS`: title, date, tags, cover, cover_alt, summary, description, slug, role, client, tools, year, link, layout, permalink, eleventyExcludeFromCollections, draft. Required: title, date, tags, cover, cover_alt.
  - `PAGE_KEYS`: title, summary, description, slug, layout, permalink, eleventyExcludeFromCollections, draft. Required: title.
  - `RESERVED_SLUGS`: tags, 404, assets, img, css, js, projects, pages, video, work, pdf. Slugs match `^[a-z0-9-]+$`. Dates are YYYY, YYYY-MM or YYYY-MM-DD.
- Real error messages to quote in the error guide (copy exact fragments from the code):
  - `image not found in assets/`, `cover not found in assets/`, `unknown tag "x" (allowed: ...)`, `unknown front matter key`, `missing front matter field`, `must be YYYY, YYYY-MM or YYYY-MM-DD`, `already used by`, `is reserved`, `"draft" must be true or false`

  - CI asset guard (scripts/check-assets.js): `over 10 MB`, `exceeds 3000px`, `unconverted .png (run npm run shrink)`
