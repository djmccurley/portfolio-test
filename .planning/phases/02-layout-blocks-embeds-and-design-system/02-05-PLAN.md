## Phase Goal

**As a** portfolio owner, **I want to** compose a rich project page by typing short block shorthand that mixes grids, full-width images, text, video and playable games, **so that** it renders as an accessible brutalist page.

<objective>
This plan lets the owner drop an .mp4 or .webm into the shared `assets/` folder, type `::: video ../../assets/name.mp4 "Title"` (optionally `full` and `poster=`), and have visitors play it with native controls, no autoplay, under any base path. Only referenced videos are published, posters are published as processed WebP, and the build, CI guard and pre-commit hook all enforce a 50 MB video cap.

Purpose: MEDIA-01 (D-07, D-09) plus the Claude's-discretion video cap and poster handling. It extends the `video` handler from plan 02-04 and the `eleventy.before`/config wiring from plans 02-01 and 02-03.
Output: lib/limits.js, lib/media.js, file-video branch in lib/embeds.js, reserved slug, eleventy.after hook, per-extension caps in scripts/check-assets.js and scripts/shrink.js, and tests.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-13 | Denial of service | Oversized videos exceeding GitHub Pages limits (100 MB per file, ~1 GB site) | medium | mitigate | 50 MB cap enforced at render (renderFileVideo), in CI (check-assets) and at commit (shrink hook); posters resized to 1600px WebP |
| T-02-14 | Information disclosure | Publishing unreferenced or original files from assets/ | medium | mitigate | Only files registered by a `::: video` block are copied; posters are processed derivatives; tests assert unreferenced videos, original posters and `_site/assets` are absent |
| T-02-15 | Tampering | Path traversal through `::: video ../../../etc/x.mp4` or poster paths | medium | mitigate | Paths resolve only through `resolveAsset`, which looks up the basename in the root assets/ index; nothing outside assets/ can be reached |
| T-02-16 | Tampering | Attribute injection through the video title | medium | mitigate | Title escaped with escapeAttr in `aria-label` and the fallback link text; file names URL-encoded with encodeURIComponent |
| T-02-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | accept | This plan installs no packages (eleventy-img is already a Phase 1 dependency); lockfile installed with `npm ci` in CI |
