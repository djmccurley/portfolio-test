## Phase Goal

**As a** portfolio owner, **I want to** compose a rich project page by typing short block shorthand that mixes grids, full-width images, text, video and playable games, **so that** it renders as an accessible brutalist page.

<objective>
Close UAT gap G-02-4: a self-hosted video with `poster=` must show its poster before playback.

Root cause (from .planning/debug/video-poster-not-shown.md, verified in headless Chrome and documented for WebKit): `renderFileVideo` in lib/media.js always appends `#t=0.001` to the video src. With `preload="metadata"` that media fragment makes the browser seek on load, which leaves poster mode and paints the video frame instead. For the owner's clip that frame is black. The fragment is the iOS first-frame trick and was only meant for blocks with no poster (02-CONTEXT.md Claude's Discretion "Posters"; research A4).

Purpose: MEDIA-01 / D-07 promise "optional poster". This plan makes the poster actually display, keeps the no-poster iOS trick, and adds regression tests on the src form, which nothing checked before. It also fixes the CI test that would otherwise fail when the owner commits a page with a `::: video` block. Without that fix the deployed iPhone spot-check needed to close the gap cannot happen.

Output: a one-branch change in lib/media.js, new and extended tests in test/video.test.js, a scoped video exemption in test/build.test.js, and 02-06-SUMMARY.md.
</objective>
